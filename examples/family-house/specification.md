# Design specification: Two-Storey Family House

Stage: concept design (RIBA Stage 2, AIA Schematic Design). Revision A, 2026-11-08.

## 1. Site strategy

The house sits 5.8 m from the street (north), 3.3 m from each side and 17 m from the rear
boundary. That leaves a deep south-facing garden. Two cars park in the front yard beside the
path to the front door. A paved terrace runs along the garden facade.

## 2. Levels and massing

- Ground floor: ±0.00, 3.0 m floor to floor.
- First floor: +3.00, 2.8 m to the eaves.
- Footprint 11.4 x 7.2 m (82.08 m² per floor, 164.16 m² in total).
- Gable roof at 35°, ridge running east-west, 0.5 m overhang; ridge at +8.63.

## 4. Circulation and cores

- The front door opens into the hall. The living room, WC and kitchen open off it, and the stair
  rises along it.
- Stair: 17 risers of 176 mm, going 262 mm, 1.0 m wide (2 x rise + going = 615 mm).
- Upstairs, the landing wraps the stair void. The bedrooms open off it at both ends of the void,
  and the void has glass guards on its open sides.
- The bathroom and en-suite sit over the hall and WC zone, for short service runs.

## 5. Openings and daylight

- Doors: 0.9 m front door; 0.8 m room doors; 0.7 m for the WC and en-suite.
- Glazing (habitable rooms): living about 34%, kitchen + dining 40%, main bedroom 25%, bedrooms 2
  and 3 about 20% of floor area. All are above the one-eighth target.

## 6. Facades and roof

White render with dark window frames. Standing-seam metal roof. Large sliding doors on the garden
facade; a calmer street facade with the front door on the centre line.

## 7. Rules and limits

Area limit 85 m² per floor: met. Setbacks: met. The engine reports 0 errors and 0 warnings.

## 8. Open issues

- Structure (walls and first floor) and foundations: engineer to confirm.
- Heating and ventilation strategy; an outdoor heat-pump position on the east side.
- Local planning rules for parking in the front yard.
